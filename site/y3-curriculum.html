lesson plans for year 3

💚
In Year 3, girls and gender-diverse children take their first steps into programming through creative, purposeful projects. Each lesson builds confidence, creativity, and the conviction that they, too, can create technology to improve the world.
💚

Super Shapes 1

🌟 In this lesson, the girls will experiment with code to discover new shapes, colors, and patterns using the Scratch Pen extension. They will learn that the best ideas emerge when they dare to experiment, ask questions, and discover new possibilities.
✨ Because every great programmer started with a simple question: "What if...?"

Super Shapes 2

Super Shapes 3

🌟 In this lesson, you will use everything you have learned to create a digital artwork with Scratch. You will experiment with shapes, colors, and patterns to express an idea, an emotion, or something important to you.
✨ Because your creativity, your imagination, and your voice can change the world... and programming is a way to share them!

Super Shapes Representation

🌟 In this lesson, you will learn about the work of Linda Liukas and explore algorithms in a fun way, using symbols, movement, and play. You will create your own instructions, test them, and discover how to improve them.
✨ Because when you feel curious, experiment, and find solutions, you can discover what is possible!
